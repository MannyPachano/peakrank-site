You do. The site files, the domain and the hosting account are in your name.

Can I change the site myself?

Yes. You get a private editor for changing text, photos and prices. The public site stays as plain, fast files with no plugins to update.

How long does a redesign take?

Most 5 page sites take 1 to 3 weeks from the first payment to launch. The main factor is how many design rounds you want.

Measurable ones, written down before work starts. For example, a top 3 map position for named searches in the towns you serve, or more calls from your Google Business Profile than on day one. If they are not met after four months, you get back all four payments.
