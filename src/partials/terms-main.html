Local SEO guarantee

Local SEO is $799 per month, billed at the start of each month, with a minimum of four months. After the fourth month, you can continue with maintenance at $399 per month, billed monthly, and cancel it before any billing date.

Before SEO work starts, we agree with you in writing on the targets the work has to reach by the end of month four. Targets are measurable results such as map pack or organic positions for named searches in named locations, calls or website visits from your Google Business Profile, or form submissions from your website. If the agreed targets are not met at the end of month four, we refund all four monthly payments, $3,196 in total, within 10 business days of your request. To use the guarantee, email hello@peakrankingseo.com within 30 days after month four ends.

The SEO guarantee applies when you have paid each month on time, given us the access the work needs (such as your Google Business Profile, Search Console and website), and have not made changes to your website, business name, address, phone number or Google Business Profile during the four months without telling us.
