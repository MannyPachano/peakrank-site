Who Moving Off Wix Or Weebly Is For

This is for owners whose builder site is slow on a phone, looks dated next to competitors or doesn't show up when people search for what they do in their town. It's also for owners who are tired of paying a builder subscription for a site they don't feel they own.

Common Problems I Find On Wix And Weebly Sites

  • Pages that load slowly on a phone because of heavy scripts, animations and full size images
  • Page titles left as the template default or as the business name alone, so Google has little to go on
  • One services page trying to cover every job, so none of them ranks
  • A free builder address like yourname.wixsite.com instead of your own domain
  • Template text and sample reviews the builder left behind
  • A phone number that can't be tapped to dial on a phone

Not every builder site has all of these, and some can be fixed inside the builder. When several show up together, a rebuild usually takes less time than patching.
