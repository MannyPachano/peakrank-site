Citations And Directories, The Unsexy Work That Matters

Google trusts businesses that have consistent information across the internet. Your name, address, and phone number (NAP) need to match exactly across Yelp, Facebook, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yellow Pages, industry-specific directories, and dozens of smaller sites that nobody thinks about but Google definitely notices. One wrong address on Yellow Pages can tank your local ranking.

This is tedious, boring, time-consuming work. Nobody wants to do it, which is exactly why it’s so valuable when I do it for you. I audit your current citations, fix the inconsistencies, and build new ones in the directories that actually matter for your industry.

How Long Does It Take?

Here’s the honest answer: 3 to 6 months to see meaningful ranking movement. Anyone who tells you they can get you to #1 in 30 days is either lying or about to get you penalized. Google intentionally moves slowly on local rankings because they want to see consistent signals over time, not quick manipulation attempts.

Month 1 is setup: audit, fixes, Google Business optimization, initial citations. Month 2-3 you’ll start seeing your profile in more searches and some initial ranking improvements. Month 4-6 is where the compound effect kicks in. More reviews, more citations, more content, and rankings start climbing for more competitive keywords. Most local markets take somewhere between six and twelve months to break into the top three, and some competitive ones take longer. You will see exactly where you stand in the monthly report either way.
