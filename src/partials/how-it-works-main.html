What Happens After You Say Yes To A New Website

This page covers every step from the free homepage mockup to the day your new site goes live: how long it takes, what I need from you, when you pay and how the money-back guarantee works. Everything happens by email or text, so you never have to set aside time for a call.

The 7 Steps From Free Mockup To Live Website

  1. You get a free homepage mockup

    Send me your current website with the mockup form. Within 3 business days I email you a link to a new homepage designed for your business. It’s free, and you don’t owe me anything for it.

  2. You pick your pages and I send a quote

    Reply with the pages you want, or ask me to suggest them. A typical small business site has 5 pages: Home, About, Services, Gallery and Contact. I reply with a one-page proposal that lists every page, the total price and the timeline. At $80 per page, 5 pages is $400.

  3. You say yes and pay the first half

    Reply “yes” to the proposal and I send an invoice for 50% of the total. For a 5 page site that’s $200.

  4. You send your logo, photos and details

    Once the first payment comes in, I send a short list of questions. Everything I need is listed further down this page. If something isn’t ready yet, send what you have and I’ll start with that.

  5. You review the full draft

    I email you a link to the full draft of every page. Look at it on your phone and your computer, then reply with the changes you want. Wording, photos, colors and layout can all change, and there’s no limit on design changes.

  6. You approve the design in writing

    When you’re happy with every page, reply “approved”. A one-line email is enough. I ask for it in writing so we both have the same record.

  7. Your site goes live and you pay the second half

    I connect your domain and put the site live in a hosting account in your name. Then I send the invoice for the other 50%, $200 for a 5 page site. Your month of unlimited support starts on launch day.

How Long A Website Redesign Takes

  • Free homepage mockup: within 3 business days of your request.
  • Replies to your emails and texts: within one business day.
  • First payment to live site: 1 to 3 weeks for most 5 page sites.

The biggest factor is how many rounds of design changes you want, and you can take as many as you need.

What I Need From You To Build The Site

  • Your logo. Any clear version works.
  • Photos. Your work, your team, your trucks or your shop. Phone photos are fine.
  • Your services. Every service you offer, with the most important ones first.
  • Your service area and hours. The towns you cover, plus any emergency or weekend availability.
  • Reviews you want shown. Tell me which ones, or send a link to where they’re posted.
  • Domain and hosting details. Where your domain is registered and who hosts your current site. If you don’t know, tell me what you do know and I’ll help you find the rest.

You don’t need to write the page text. I write it from your answers, and you can change anything in the draft.

When You Pay For A Website Redesign

  • Free homepage mockup: nothing.
  • When you say yes to the proposal: 50% of the total, $200 for a 5 page site.
  • When your site goes live: the other 50%, $200 for a 5 page site.

The price is $80 per designed page, and the proposal shows the full total before you pay anything. Two costs aren’t part of that price: hosting fees, which you pay the host directly (most small business sites fit on a free hosting plan), and your domain registration, which you keep paying to your registrar as you do today.

The Money-Back Guarantee On Your Website Design

You can ask for as many design changes as you need. If at any point before you approve the design you’re not happy with it, email hello@peakrankingseo.com and tell me. I cancel the project and refund everything you’ve paid within 10 business days.

Once you’ve approved the design and the site is live, the website payments aren’t refundable. Read the full guarantee terms.

What You Own And The Support You Get After Launch

The site files, the domain and the hosting account are in your name, so you can move the site to any host you like. You also get a simple editor to change text, photos and prices yourself.

For one month after launch, I make fixes and changes to the pages you paid for at no extra cost, with no limit on the number of requests. New pages added after the design is approved are $80 each.
